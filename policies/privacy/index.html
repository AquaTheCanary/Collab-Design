Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Collab Design ("we", "our", or "us"), a service provided by Convex Company. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our web-based vector collaboration engine and workspace platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering for an account, expressing interest in obtaining information about us, or using our services.

  • Account Information: Name, work email address, legal organization name, job role, and profile picture (avatar).
  • Single Sign-On (SSO) Data: When authenticating via Google Workspace or SAML providers, we collect your name, email address, and profile credentials authorized by your provider.
  • Design & Workspace Content: Vector graphic elements, shape coordinates, project titles, canvas assets, and annotations created within the Vector Studio Editor.
  • Log and Usage Data: Service-related, diagnostic, usage, and performance information collected automatically when accessing our application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our contract with you, and compliance with our legal obligations:

  • To facilitate account creation, authentication, and secure login processes.
  • To deliver multi-user real-time vector canvas rendering and workspace synchronization.
  • To send administrative verification codes (such as SMTP OTP codes during organization setup) and system notifications.
  • To enforce domain verification policies and prevent public webmail security abuse on enterprise accounts.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers solely to facilitate operating our platform:

  • Authentication Providers: Google OAuth 2.0 and enterprise SAML IDPs for Single Sign-On credentials.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting: Vercel serverless platform functions and Convex real-time backend persistence.

5. Data Retention and Security

We maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against accidental, unlawful, or unauthorized destruction, loss, alteration, access, or disclosure. Account information and workspace assets are retained for as long as your account remains active.

6. Your Rights and Contact Us

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.
